09 / QUESTIONS

What you should know.

Does this replace AMPECO, Driivz or my existing charging platform?

No. Your platform continues running chargers and the charging service.

Is the adviser connected to live charger data?

Not in the standard pilot. We start with agreed exports and a repeatable refresh. Live connection requires feasibility review and separate scope.

Can it automatically change charging schedules?

No. The initial pilot is read-only. Your team reviews recommendations and remains responsible for decisions.

Do we need Claude or ChatGPT?

We agree the workspace after discovery. You may need your own suitable subscription. Third-party fees are separate.

Will our data be used to train AI models?

We document the provider, account type, settings and data handling before access. Policies differ by tool and subscription.

What if our data is incomplete?

We check suitability, narrow the question, identify missing fields or recommend not proceeding. Uncertainty remains visible.

Does this guarantee lower electricity costs or higher revenue?

No. Commercial benefit depends on data, decisions and operating conditions.

Who owns the finished tool?

The intended model is an agreed client-owned workspace. Ownership, reusable components and licences are specified in the proposal.

What happens after handover?

Refresh instructions and a 14-day correction window. Maintenance, extra work and live integrations are separate.
